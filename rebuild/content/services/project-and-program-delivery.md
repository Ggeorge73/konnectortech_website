## The join this service owns

Most programs have a plan, a budget and a team. What they often lack is one party that answers for all three at once. When a milestone slips, each vendor points at another, and the client is left to referee.

We take that seat. You get one partner that owns the plan, runs the delivery and reports the truth about where it stands.

## What you receive

- **A plan everyone agrees up front.** Scope, schedule, budget and team, with what “accepted” means written down before work starts.
- **Delivery leadership.** Project and program management that keeps every milestone honest.
- **A working rhythm.** Agile and SAFe delivery where it fits, and a simpler cadence where it does not.
- **PMO support.** Reporting, controls and governance your sponsors can rely on.
- **Risk handled early.** Risks are named, owned and tracked, so a surprise changes the route and not the destination.

## How it connects to the rest

Delivery is the core of what we do. Integration, staffing and sourcing exist to serve it, so you manage one partner instead of many vendors.
