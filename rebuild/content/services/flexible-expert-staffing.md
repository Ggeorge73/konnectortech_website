## The join this service owns

Projects stall when the right skill is missing for a few months and hiring for it would take longer than the work itself. The gap between the team you have and the team the project needs is one more place delivery fails.

We fill that gap with skilled consultants who join your team and work to your plan.

## What you receive

- **The right skill at the right stage.** Consultants matched to the work in front of you.
- **Capacity that moves with the project.** Scale up for the build, scale down after handover.
- **No permanent headcount.** You pay for expertise while you need it.
- **One accountable partner.** Our consultants work inside the same delivery method, so staffing never becomes a second vendor to manage.

## How it connects to the rest

Staffing supports delivery. It keeps the plan moving when the work changes shape, which is one way we keep projects on budget.
