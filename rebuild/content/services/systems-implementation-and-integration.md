## The join this service owns

A system can pass every test by itself and still fail the organization, because the failure sits between systems: the finance platform that cannot read the records system, or the vendor product that expects data the legacy system never held.

We own those joins. A connector is the piece that makes separate parts work as one, and that is where our name comes from.

## What you receive

- **Configuration that fits how you work.** Systems set up against your processes and your acceptance criteria.
- **Integrations that are owned.** Every handoff between systems has a named owner, a test and a fallback.
- **A planned cutover.** Data moved, checked and reconciled before go-live.
- **A rollout people can follow.** Training and support so the new system is used, not worked around.

## How it connects to the rest

Integration runs inside our delivery method. It is planned in stage two, built in stage three and proven in stage four against the criteria you agreed.
