## The join this service owns

A system can pass every test by itself and still fail the organization. The failure sits between systems: a finance platform that cannot read the records system, or a vendor product that expects data the legacy system never held.

We own those joins. A connector is the piece that makes separate parts work as one, and that is where our name comes from.

## What you receive

- **Configuration that fits how you work.** Systems set up against your processes and your acceptance criteria.
- **Connections that are owned.** Every handoff between systems has an owner.
- **A rollout people can follow.** Trained people, so the new system is used and not worked around.

## How it connects to the rest

Integration runs inside our delivery method. It is planned in stage two, built in stage three and proven in stage four against the criteria you agreed.
