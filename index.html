Breakthrough technology only matters once it works inside your environment. We do not invent these devices. We close the gaps between the maker's device and your systems, security, compliance, people and rollout: the same gaps every project fails in.

Public sectorDraft · wording and permission to confirm

Koniku Konikore

What it is
Koniku describes Konikore as a smell processor: living neurons on a chip that read molecules in the air, built to detect explosive and chemical threats.
Where it fits
Defense, aviation and border screening, critical infrastructure.
What Konnector does to land it
  1. Assessment: where detection is needed and what it has to connect to.
  2. Systems and data: alerts routed into your screening and security systems.
  3. Compliance path: the approvals your authority requires, mapped and tracked.
  4. Training: operators and supervisors ready before day one.
  5. Go-live: tested against the acceptance criteria you set.

QVITI tES devices

What it is
QVITI makes non-invasive brain stimulation (tES) devices for clinical therapy, rehabilitation and research. It lists CorStim and nuroStym today, with QStim and VRstim to come.
Where it fits
Hospitals, rehabilitation clinics and research centres.
What Konnector does to land it
  1. Assessment: clinical or research use, the setting and who will run it.
  2. Systems and data: session records into your clinical or research systems.
  3. Compliance path: your medical device, privacy and ethics requirements.
  4. Training: clinicians and technicians ready before day one.
  5. Go-live: tested against the acceptance criteria you set.

RSQ HOLO AR goggles for the operating room

What it is
RSQ HOLO describes its product as an augmented operating room: goggles that place 3D imaging and patient record (EMR) information in the surgeon's view.
Where it fits
Hospitals and surgical centres.
What Konnector does to land it
  1. Assessment: theatres, imaging sources and the network they run on.
  2. Systems and data: imaging and patient record feeds reaching the goggles securely.
  3. Compliance path: your medical device, privacy and infection control requirements.
  4. Training: surgical teams and IT ready before day one.
  5. Go-live: tested against the acceptance criteria you set.
