Technology sourcing

Where a project needs it, we source and integrate the technology, so you deal with one partner, not many vendors.

  • Hardware and software a project needs
  • Sourced as part of delivery
  • Integrated by the same partner
  • One partner, not many vendors

The join this service owns

When one firm supplies the technology and another delivers the project, the client stands in the middle. Late shipments, licence gaps and “that is not in our scope” all land on the client’s desk.

Where a project needs it, we source the technology and integrate it ourselves, so the handoff between supplier and delivery team is ours to manage.

What you receive

  • Technology that matches the plan. What is sourced is what the project needs, when it needs it.
  • One line of accountability. The partner that buys the technology is the partner that makes it work.
  • Fewer contracts to manage. You deal with one partner, not many vendors.

How it connects to the rest

We source technology only as part of a delivery. It is never a standalone resale offer.
